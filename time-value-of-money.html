Increasing Cash Flows

Increasing (or growing) cash flows follow a geometric series where each payment grows by a fixed percentage g per period. The present value of a growing annuity is:

PV = A ×
1 − ((1+g)/(1+i))N / i − g

where A is the first payment, i is the discount rate, g is the growth rate, and N is the number of periods.

Q18. Approach 1:

b.
